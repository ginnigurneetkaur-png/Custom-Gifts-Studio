NEED A LITTLE HELP?

Custom gift FAQs.

Quick answers to help you place your order.

How do I place a custom order?

Choose your gift in the custom-order form, describe your idea, then send the request using WhatsApp.

Can I send a photo or design?

Yes. Choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP image in the order form. Attach the selected image manually to your WhatsApp message.

How much will my custom gift cost?

Pricing depends on the product you choose and the customization details you request. We will confirm the final price and delivery details after reviewing your order.

Can I request a different gift or design?

Choose “Other Custom Gift” and describe what you have in mind. We will let you know what we can make.
